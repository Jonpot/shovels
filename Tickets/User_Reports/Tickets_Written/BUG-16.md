A complaint about stack fanning:

The stack fanning itself is very good- once it's started. But the "hitbox" to start the fanning should extend from the top of the stack to where the bottom of the stack is rendered.

Right now, I can have my mouse fully hovered over the bottom of the card lowest on the screen in a stack and the stack does not "zoom" to fan. This causes very glitchy behavior sometimes where the stack flickers between being zoomed and being compressed.
