Having to click on a character first before selecting cards to play is cumbersome. While I can understand the scenarios when this would be necessary (in the case of wanting to tap a character instead of playing cards), 90% of turns are just playing cards directly from characters. Clicking on a card in a character's stack should automatically select that character as the source for playing the card, streamlining the process.

Furthermore, right now, if I click on a card in a character's stack, it prevents me from selecting that card unless I'm selecting the "topmost" card or a card adjacent to a previously selected card. This is correct behavior according to the rules, but it is cumbersome to select many cards at once, requiring multiple clicks up from the top of the stack. Instead, clicking on any card in a character's stack should select all cards above it as well, allowing for quicker multi-card selections.
